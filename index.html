About

A family-run builder, with an architect’s eye.

Wandle started in 2009 with one van, one carpenter and a yard off Garratt Lane. Seventeen years later we are thirty-eight people — architects, engineers and our own site teams — and we still only build in south west London.

We named the business after the river that runs from Croydon to Wandsworth, through almost every postcode we work in. Most of our clients live within a few streets of a house we have already built, and most of our work comes from neighbours who have watched us on site.

Keeping design, engineering and building under one roof means one contract, one programme and one person accountable from the first measured survey to the day we hand back the keys.
